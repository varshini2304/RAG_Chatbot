## 1. Company Overview
Apex Healthcare & Wellness is a premier, non-profit healthcare organization dedicated to providing patient-centered clinical care, specialized surgical interventions, and preventive wellness programs. Established in 2005, Apex operates the Apex Memorial Hospital, a state-of-the-art facility featuring Complex A (Inpatient & Trauma Center) and Complex B (Outpatient Clinics & Research Labs) in Chicago, Illinois. With over 800 beds and a team of 3,500 clinical and administrative professionals, Apex provides advanced diagnostic testing, emergency medicine, intensive care, and specialized oncology, cardiology, and pediatric services.



## 2. Vision & Mission
* **Our Vision:** To lead the healthcare industry in clinical quality, medical innovation, and patient experience, creating healthier communities one patient at a time.
* **Our Mission:** To deliver compassionate, evidence-based medical and surgical care of the highest standard, respecting patient dignity, privacy, and safety while fostering medical research and clinical education.
