## 23. Internal Project Details
Apex is actively executing several clinical improvement initiatives:
* **Operation HeartCare:** An initiative targeting zero readmissions for cardiac bypass patients. Operation HeartCare deploys remote monitoring tablets to post-operative patients at home. Lead: Dr. Evelyn Thorne. Primary EHR integration server: **EPIC-EHR-03**.
* **ApexCare-Portal:** The design and launch of our next-generation patient portal, allowing patients to schedule clinical visits, view lab results, and message doctors. Lead: Gregory Vance. Database Server: **APEX-PATIENT-DB**.
* **Project MediConnect-v2.1:** Upgrading our hospital EHR network to support HL7 FHIR protocols for seamless medical data sharing. Lead: Dr. Emily Chen.



## 24. Product Documentation
Apex Healthcare provides medical and clinical services directly to patients, including:
* **Apex Specialized Telehealth Service:** A secure video platform for remote medical consultations, operating under HIPAA security controls. Version: v2.5.
* **Cardiac Care Plan:** A standardized, multidisciplinary clinical protocol for managing congestive heart failure patients, combining medication tracking and dietary regimens.
* **Outpatient Physical Therapy Program:** Personalized rehabilitation regimens for joint recovery, conducted at our Complex B clinic.
