## 25. Customer Support Process
Vanguard provides technical B2B customer support for automotive assembly plants:
1. **Support Ingestion:** Quality issues are reported via email `careers@vanguardautotech.com` (Quality Desk) or direct hotline (Ext. 7789).
2. **Action Plan:** Within 2 hours of receiving a defect report, a Quality Engineer is assigned to perform root-cause analysis.
3. **Containment:** If a defect is confirmed, we issue a containment notice to hold all shipped inventory in transit.
4. **Resolution:** A physical replacement batch is manufactured and expedited to the customer site within 48 hours.
