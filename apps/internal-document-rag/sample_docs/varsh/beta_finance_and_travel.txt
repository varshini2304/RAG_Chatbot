## 21. Travel Policy
**Policy Number:** #FIN-TR-BETA-44. Travel for vendor audits, equipment inspections, or automotive conferences must be authorized by the COO. Driving personal vehicles for business is reimbursed at the standard rate of $0.65 per mile. Lodging is capped at $150 per night. Employees must book all travel via the Vanguard Travel Desk.



## 22. Expense Reimbursement
Reimbursement claims must be submitted using the paper Expense Voucher Form EV-1, accompanied by original physical receipts. Vouchers must be signed by the department supervisor and submitted to the Accounts Payable department by the 10th of the month. Reimbursements are paid out on the next payroll cycle.
