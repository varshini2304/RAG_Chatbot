## 1. Company Overview
Aetheris AI Systems is a premier global software product enterprise specializing in cutting-edge artificial intelligence models, cloud services, and machine learning infrastructure. Founded in 2018 in Seattle, Washington, Aetheris has grown from a specialized research lab into an industry-leading cloud platform provider. We serve over 15,000 enterprise clients globally, offering scalable SaaS solutions that embed deep neural networking capabilities directly into enterprise operations. Our primary engineering and research facilities are located in our state-of-the-art facility, the Seattle Aetheris Tower, occupying floors 20 through 35.



## 2. Vision & Mission
* **Our Vision:** To democratize superintelligence, creating a seamless cloud fabric where cognitive models optimize every business decision in real-time, empowering humanity to solve complex global challenges.
* **Our Mission:** To build the world's most robust, ethical, and scalable artificial intelligence platforms, delivering reliable, high-throughput cloud infrastructure that simplifies model deployment and operations for enterprises of all sizes.
